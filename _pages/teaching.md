    <h3>Mutation-Induced Drug Resistance in EGFR</h3>
    <em>Poster presentation at: <strong>Markey Cancer Center Research Day (MCCRD) 2025</strong>, Lexington, KY</em>
    <p>
      Our poster detailed how specific EGFR mutations form additional hydrogen bonds, leading to drug resistance. This work combines molecular dynamics with structural bioinformatics to inform future inhibitor design.
    </p>
