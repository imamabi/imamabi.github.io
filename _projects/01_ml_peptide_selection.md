This project integrates protein language models with molecular dynamics simulations to discover and validate functional peptides, including antifreeze and antibiofouling candidates. PLMs support rapid sequence prioritization and diversity selection, while explicit-solvent MD simulations evaluate conformational stability, interfacial behavior, and functional motifs before experimental follow-up.
