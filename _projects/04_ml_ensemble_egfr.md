This project develops an ensemble ML architecture with late fusion across complementary molecular representations, model scores, and cheminformatics features to identify potential hERG and CYP3K binders. The workflow emphasizes model calibration, evaluation across target-specific endpoints, and prioritization of chemically tractable candidates for follow-up review.
