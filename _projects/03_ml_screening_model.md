This project involved the development and implementation of a robust computational model for high-throughput screening of peptide libraries. The goal was to rapidly identify and prioritize novel antibiofouling peptide candidates, significantly reducing the time and cost associated with experimental screening methods. The model successfully pinpointed several promising sequences for further validation.
