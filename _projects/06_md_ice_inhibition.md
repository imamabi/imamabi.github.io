Through large-scale molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, this study explored the molecular mechanism of ice growth inhibition by antifreeze peptides. We analyzed how these peptides bind to ice crystal surfaces, disrupting the crystallization process and providing a foundation for designing new cryoprotectant agents.
