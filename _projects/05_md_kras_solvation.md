This research uses all-atom molecular dynamics to probe how sequence variation and oncogenic mutations alter structural dynamics and function in EGFR, KRAS, and p53. The analyses connect conformational ensembles, hydration, hydrogen-bond networks, nucleotide or ligand binding, and mutation-driven interaction changes to biochemical phenotypes and inhibitor-design hypotheses.
