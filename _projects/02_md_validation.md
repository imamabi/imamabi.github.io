Following the computational screening, this project used all-atom molecular dynamics (MD) simulations to validate the interfacial properties of the most promising antibiofouling peptide candidates. The simulations provided detailed insights into the peptide's behavior at material-water interfaces, confirming their stability and mechanism of action before experimental synthesis.
